1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of RIGHT CLICK CUSTOMER WORKBOOK (the “Service”) handles information when a customer uses the Service, including when the customer chooses to connect a Google Account. The Service is referred to as “RIGHT CLICK ENGINE” in this policy.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features a customer uses, the Service may process the following information:

  • Account and registration details, such as a customer identifier and registered email address.
  • License and account-status information used to determine whether access is permitted.
  • Email address and verification information used during email OTP authentication.
  • Google Account identity information returned during Google sign-in/authorization, such as the Google account subject identifier and verified email address.
  • The Google Spreadsheet ID selected by the customer, and workbook metadata or data needed to verify the authorized connection.
  • Session, challenge, security, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the Service.

3. Google Account and Google Sheets access

If a customer chooses to connect a Google Account, the Service uses Google OAuth to request the permissions displayed on Google's consent screen. Depending on the configured authorization flow, these permissions may include OpenID identity, the customer's Google Account email address, and read-only access to Google Sheets.

The intended workbook connection in this Service is read-only. The Service should not modify a customer's spreadsheet through this OAuth connection. Customers should review the permissions shown by Google before granting access.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • verify a customer's identity, registered email address, and eligibility to use the Service;
  • authenticate and maintain customer sessions;
  • process a customer-requested Google Account connection;
  • identify and verify the selected spreadsheet and permitted read access;
  • protect the Service, investigate errors, prevent misuse, and provide support; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Google APIs and service providers

When a customer authorizes Google access, information is processed through Google services and APIs required for authentication and the requested Sheets operation. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy terms and policies. The Service does not control Google's systems.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers used to host or operate the Service, to the extent necessary to provide its functions. The operator should identify any additional providers used by the deployed system before final publication.

6. Sharing and sale

The operator does not sell customers' personal information. Information is not disclosed to third parties except as needed to operate the Service, when authorized by the customer, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of the Service and its users.

7. Security

The Service is designed to use access checks, authentication, and limited Google permissions to protect customer information. No internet-based service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should protect their email and Google accounts and notify the operator if they suspect unauthorized use.

8. Retention and deletion requests

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, security, support, and legal or business requirements. Actual retention periods may differ by information type and system component. Customers may contact the operator to ask about information associated with their account or to request deletion. Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal, security, backup, and operational requirements.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business and software customers and is not designed for children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Service or its information-handling practices change. The revised policy will be published at this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
